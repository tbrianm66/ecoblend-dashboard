You are acting as a senior full-stack product engineer and venture studio systems architect.

I am building **EcoBLEND IO**, a Venture Validation OS used by **EcoRACE Studio** to manage and validate multiple portfolio ventures using Lean Startup methodology, venture readiness scoring, evidence capture, and stage-gate decision-making.

The current application has a sidebar module:

**1. Command Centre**

It currently includes the following sub-sections:

1. Portfolio Overview
2. Command Centre
3. Opportunity Pipeline
4. Venture Status
5. Alerts & Approvals

Your task is to update the **Command Centre module** so it becomes the central Lean Startup decision cockpit for all ventures.

The Command Centre must not be a static dashboard. It must aggregate evidence from Venture Intake, Discovery & Market, Proposition & Model, R&D Hub, Operations & MFG, Brand & GTM, and Market Risk Log.

Its purpose is to help the venture studio answer:

**“Which ventures should persevere, pivot, pause, kill, or advance to the next validation stage?”**

# 1. Core Product Principle

The Command Centre should operate around the Lean Startup loop:

**Hypothesis → Experiment → Evidence → Learning → Decision → Next Action**

Every venture should have:

* Active hypotheses
* Active experiments
* Evidence scores
* Risk scores
* Stage-gate status
* Decision recommendations
* Alerts requiring founder or operator action

Do not optimise for visual dashboard theatre. Optimise for decision quality.

Create or update the Command Centre page to include a **Lean Decision Board**.

* Venture name
* Current hypothesis

* Top contradicting evidence
* Risk score

## Decision logic

Generate recommendations using the following rules:

If evidence confidence is below 40:

* Recommend: Evidence needed

If evidence confidence is 40–59:

* Recommend: Continue testing

If evidence confidence is 60 or above but WTP score is below 40:

* Recommend: Pivot value proposition or pricing

If evidence confidence is 60 or above and risk score is above 75:

* Recommend: Pause until risk is mitigated

If evidence confidence is below 30 and risk score is above 75:

* Recommend: Kill or major pivot

If evidence confidence is above 75 and risk score is below 50:

* Recommend: Ready to advance

If a hypothesis is invalidated:

* Recommend: Pivot

## Required actions

The user should be able to:

* Open decision details
* Approve recommendation
* Reject recommendation
* Add reviewer notes
* Create follow-up experiment
* Create pivot log
* Create stage-gate review

## Stage definitions

Use these stages:

1. Intake
2. Problem Validation
3. Market Validation
4. Commercial Validation
5. MVP Validation
6. Delivery Validation
7. GTM Validation
8. Investment Ready

## Stage-gate rules

A venture may not progress from Intake to Problem Validation unless:

* At least one problem hypothesis exists
* At least one customer segment hypothesis exists
* At least one riskiest assumption is defined

A venture may not progress from Problem Validation to Market Validation unless:

* Customer Discovery Score is 60 or above
* At least five customer interviews exist
* At least one current workaround is identified

A venture may not progress from Market Validation to Commercial Validation unless:

* Competitor Mapping is completed
* Demand Signal Score is 60 or above
* Market Risk Log has no unresolved critical market risk

A venture may not progress from Commercial Validation to MVP Validation unless:

* WTP Score is 60 or above
* Budget owner status is known
* Pricing model hypothesis exists

A venture may not progress from MVP Validation to Delivery Validation unless:

* MVP experiment completed
* Prototype or concierge MVP result captured
* Solution hypothesis updated with learning

A venture may not progress from Delivery Validation to GTM Validation unless:

* Supplier or operational readiness is assessed
* Cost-to-deliver is estimated
* Major operational risks have mitigation plans

A venture may not progress from GTM Validation to Investment Ready unless:

* GTM experiment evidence exists
* Repeatable channel hypothesis exists
* Commercial model is validated
* Critical risks are mitigated
* Evidence summary is exportable

## Required actions

The user should be able to:

* Open stage-gate checklist
* Request review
* Approve progression
* Reject progression
* Add conditional approval
* Create required actions
* Generate stage-gate decision summary

# 19. Critical Failure Modes to Avoid

Avoid these implementation mistakes:

1. **Dashboard theatre**
   Do not create attractive cards without operational decision logic.

2. **Disconnected forms**
   Hypotheses, experiments, evidence, decisions, risks, and alerts must link together.

3. **Premature progression**
   Ventures should not advance stages without evidence warnings.

4. **False validation**
   Positive customer comments should not equal commercial validation.

5. **No pivot memory**
   Every pivot must be recorded with evidence and rationale.

6. **No cross-module integration**
   The Command Centre must aggregate from Discovery & Market, Proposition & Model, R&D Hub, Operations & MFG, Brand & GTM, and Venture Intake.

7. **Overbuilding before testing**
   Build the Command Centre as an evidence and decision system first, not as a complex reporting suite.
