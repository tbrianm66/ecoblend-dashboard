You are acting as a senior full-stack product architect, database auditor, and governance workflow specialist.

I want you to audit the existing database platform and application at:

https://www.ecoblend.studio

Objective:
Audit the current platform, database schema, navigation, modules, workflows, data models, permissions, and UI functionality to identify gaps and improvement opportunities required to capture a new “Purpose-Locked Governance Workflow” for protecting a company’s mission, ethics, capital structure, and long-term purpose.

This workflow is based on the following governance logic:

1. A company should define a protected purpose.
2. That purpose should be translated into a company charter, articles, board pledge, investor policy, reserved matters, and operational metrics.
3. The platform should track whether company decisions remain aligned with the purpose.
4. The system should detect mission drift, capital pressure, governance weakness, and stakeholder risk.
5. Corrective governance actions should be triggered when red flags are detected.
6. The platform should support recurring reviews: monthly management review, quarterly purpose review, annual constitutional review.
7. The workflow should protect against:
   - mission drift
   - hostile or misaligned capital
   - boardroom betrayal
   - weak succession planning
   - misuse or sale of IP
   - metric distortion
   - governance documents becoming decorative rather than operational
   - shareholder pressure overriding the original purpose

Core workflow to support:

Founder Purpose Definition
→ Company Purpose Charter
→ Beneficial Purpose Statement
→ Mission Lock Design
→ Governance Structure Selection
→ Protected Articles / Constitution
→ Board Mission Pledge
→ Director Duties & Reserved Matters
→ Investor Rights Framework
→ Capital Alignment Decision
→ Governance Operating System
→ Monthly / Quarterly / Annual Reviews
→ Purpose Drift Detection
→ Corrective Governance Action
→ Board / Steward / Founder Review
→ Strategy, Incentive or Capital-Term Correction
→ Annual Constitutional Review
→ Return to Purpose Charter

Please audit the platform against this workflow.

B. APPLICATION MODULE AUDIT

Audit the current module structure and determine whether the platform has the right modules to support this governance workflow.

Required modules:

1. Governance Command Centre
   Purpose:
   Central dashboard showing company purpose, governance maturity, red flags, review dates, investor alignment, reserved matters, and corrective actions.

2. Purpose Charter Builder
   Purpose:
   Guided workflow for creating and versioning the company’s protected purpose, founder intent, stakeholder commitments, and non-negotiable principles.

3. Mission Lock Designer
   Purpose:
   Tool for selecting and documenting the governance structure required to protect the company purpose.

4. Constitutional Governance Module
   Purpose:
   Tracks articles, shareholder agreements, reserved matters, board powers, voting rights, supermajority protections, and amendment controls.

5. Board and Stewardship Module
   Purpose:
   Tracks directors, stewards, board mission pledges, conflicts of interest, mission alignment, voting rights, and board review obligations.

6. Investor Alignment Module
   Purpose:
   Screens investors for mission alignment, control risk, exit pressure, debt risk, and governance compatibility.

7. Reserved Matters Register
   Purpose:
   Records decisions that require special approval, such as sale of company, sale of IP, new investors, debt, major pivots, purpose amendments, or founder removal.

8. Purpose Metrics Dashboard
   Purpose:
   Tracks purpose KPIs, financial sustainability, people/culture indicators, stakeholder impact, sustainability metrics, capital pressure, IP protection, and governance risk.

9. Purpose Drift Detection Module
   Purpose:
   Detects red flags and classifies them by risk category, severity, evidence, owner, and required escalation.

10. Corrective Action Tracker
   Purpose:
   Tracks corrective governance actions from detection through board review, approval, implementation, and closure.

11. Governance Document Vault
   Purpose:
   Stores and versions all critical governance documents, including articles, board pledges, shareholder agreements, investor policies, reserved matters schedules, IP policies, and social mission policies.

12. Annual Constitutional Review Module
   Purpose:
   Forces annual review of purpose, structure, board, capital, reserved matters, and governance controls.
