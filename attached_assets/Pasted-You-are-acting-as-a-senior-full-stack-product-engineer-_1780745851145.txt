You are acting as a senior full-stack product engineer, venture studio systems architect, and Lean Startup product strategist.

C. Discovery & Market

WTP Assessment

WTP means Willingness To Pay.

Your task is to audit the existing WTP Assessment module, identify gaps, and then develop it into a structured Lean Startup commercial validation system.

The WTP Assessment module must not measure whether customers “like the idea”. It must test whether customers will commit:

* Money
* Budget
* Procurement time
* Operational data
* Pilot access
* Internal stakeholder introductions
* Letters of intent
* Paid trials
* Co-development resources

The purpose of this module is to answer:

“Is there credible commercial evidence that this customer segment will pay for this value proposition?”

5. WTP Evidence Ladder

Implement a clear evidence ladder.

This should be visible in the WTP Assessment module.

Evidence levels:

1. Customer says “interesting”
2. Customer agrees to another meeting
3. Customer shares internal or operational data
4. Customer introduces a budget holder
5. Customer requests a proposal
6. Customer signs an LOI or formal pilot agreement
7. Customer pays for a pilot or purchase order

Display:

* Level number
* Evidence description
* Evidence strength
* What it proves
* What it does not prove

Important interpretation:

Level 1 and 2 are not WTP validation. They are weak interest signals.

Level 3 is useful because data-sharing indicates operational commitment.

Level 4 validates access to budget authority.

Level 5 validates buying-process movement.

Level 6 validates formal intent.

Level 7 validates real willingness to pay.

7. Scoring Logic

Implement reusable scoring utilities.

Evidence Level Score

Map evidence level to base score:

* Level 1 = 10
* Level 2 = 25
* Level 3 = 40
* Level 4 = 55
* Level 5 = 70
* Level 6 = 85
* Level 7 = 100

WTP Score

Calculate:

WTP Score =
Evidence Level Score × 50%

* Budget Owner Score × 20%
* Procurement Pathway Score × 15%
* Pricing Response Score × 15%

Where:

Budget Owner Score:

* Confirmed = 100
* Partially known = 50
* Unknown = 0

Procurement Pathway Score:

* Validated = 100
* Feasible = 75
* Mapped = 60
* Unknown = 25
* Blocked = 0

Pricing Response Score:

* Accepted = 100
* Negotiating = 75
* Needs ROI proof = 50
* Price resistance = 25
* Rejected = 0

Cap final score between 0 and 100.

Interpretation

* 0–39 = Weak WTP
* 40–59 = Early commercial signal
* 60–79 = Strong commercial signal
* 80–100 = High-confidence WTP

Display this interpretation on the UI.

8. WTP Decision Logic

Generate a decision recommendation for each WTP test.

If WTP Score is 80 or above:

* Recommend: proceed to paid pilot, LOI, or MVP validation

If WTP Score is 60–79:

* Recommend: strengthen commercial evidence and clarify procurement pathway

If WTP Score is 40–59:

* Recommend: run another pricing or budget-owner test

If WTP Score is below 40:

* Recommend: do not proceed to MVP build; pivot value proposition, customer segment, or pricing model

If evidence level is 1 or 2:

* Warning: positive interest is not commercial validation

If budget owner is unknown:

* Warning: WTP is not validated until economic buyer or budget owner is identified

If procurement pathway is blocked:

* Warning: adoption may be blocked even if buyer interest exists

If the customer is willing to share data but not pay:

* Recommend testing data partnership, co-development, or paid pilot structure

Risk title: Budget owner unknown

Severity: high

If procurement pathway is blocked or unknown:

Risk title: Procurement pathway unclear or blocked
Risk category: procurement_risk
Severity: high

If main objection is data_sharing_concern:

Risk title: Data sharing may block commercial validation
Risk category: data_access_risk
Severity: high

If main objection is trust_barrier:

Risk title: Customer trust barrier may block purchase
Risk category: trust_risk
Severity: medium or high

Auto-create Command Alerts

If a venture is moved to MVP Validation but WTP Score is below 60:

Alert type: low_wtp
Severity: critical
Recommended action: Do not proceed to MVP build until commercial validation is strengthened

If a WTP next action is overdue:

Alert type: approval_required or overdue_commercial_action
Severity: high

If a proposal has been requested but no follow-up action exists:

Alert type: approval_required
Severity: medium

22. Critical Failure Modes to Avoid

Avoid these mistakes:

1. Treating customer enthusiasm as willingness to pay.
2. Allowing WTP records without a linked venture or hypothesis.
3. Creating pricing forms without procurement or budget-owner context.
4. Ignoring enterprise sales friction.
5. Failing to distinguish buyer, user, budget owner, and decision-maker.
6. Creating disconnected WTP data that does not feed Command Centre.
7. Allowing MVP progression with weak commercial validation.
8. Overcomplicating the UI so the user cannot see the next commercial action.
9. Duplicating venture or hypothesis tables.
10. Failing to record objections and reasons for non-purchase.
